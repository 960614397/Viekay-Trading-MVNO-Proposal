A fixed line dies with the grid. A phone doesn't.

Viekay already delivers essential connectivity infrastructure across four towns — the kind of public-facing digital-inclusion mandate municipalities depend on. A Viekay Mobile SIM turns Viekay's reach into a connection that survives both — the property line and the power outage — on infrastructure Viekay already owns: the customer relationship, the town-by-town trust, and the brand.

02 · Right to Win

Why Viekay wins in mobile

This is not a me-too MVNO. Viekay brings four structural advantages no telco or reseller can replicate.

🔌 The power-outage pitch no fixed ISP can make

Every Viekay customer already knows what happens to a fixed line when the grid goes down. Viekay is the one operator that can turn that shared frustration into the sale — "the connection that survives the power cut" — and it's a line no MNO retail store, with no fixed-line business to protect, would ever think to say.

🔋 Rewards that become reach

Only Viekay can route mobile rewards straight into a growing Viekay Vault and bank spare data instead of losing it — turning a personal SIM into the top-ups, usage and engagement an established multi-town ISP actually needs.

📱 App + town-office distribution

Viekay's town offices and installation teams already reach every connected household and business across its footprint. SIM sign-up, RICA and self-care nest inside a channel that already acquires customer-by-customer — near-zero incremental CAC.

🤝 Trust, and a public-infrastructure mandate

Residents already trust Viekay enough to let it wire the home or business. Reward-funded data lowers the effective price without a tariff war — and it extends the same digital-inclusion mandate that built the fixed network in the first place, on a personal SIM billed to the same account.

06 · Business Case

Profitable within year 1 — the cross-sell case

Viekay doesn't need to build a customer base first — it already has one: ~180,000 residential and business ISP customers live across four towns today, with a fifth in the pipeline. The MVNO activates a personal SIM into that existing relationship town by town, the same way Viekay rolled out its fixed network in the first place. Modelled over 60 months at 5% monthly churn across six audience-built CVPs — GO, HUSTLE, SHIELD, FAM, ROAM and CROWN — reaching a conservative ~25% cross-sell attach (45,000 active subscribers) by year 5. Blended ARPU ~R283, lifted by HUSTLE and ROAM's business-grade usage, across six CVPs costed off the real MTN wholesale rate card at a ≥50% gross-margin floor.
